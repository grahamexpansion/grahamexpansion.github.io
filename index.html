The approach

Three pillars.

Voice. Positioning. Content strategy. The brand that lets the business be known beyond the service — and lets the founder be hired before they pitch.

Digital and physical alike. Books, software, apparel, hardware — anything the brand can carry beyond the service. Products that earn while the founder isn't trading hours for them.

Operations

Team, systems, and infrastructure that operate without the founder. The difference between a practice that earns and a company that runs.
